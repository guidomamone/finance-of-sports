**Introduction**

The principal activity of McLaren Racing Limited (the "Company") during the year was participating in the Formula One ("F1") motor racing series. This includes the design, development, manufacture and racing of F1 cars.

**Business review**

**Racing season and financial key performance indicators:**

2025 saw McLaren retain the Formula One Constructors' Championship, marking its first back-to-back Championship win since 1991, as well as Lando Norris securing his maiden Drivers' Championship, the first for McLaren since 2008. Between the two of them, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri secured 14 race wins across the season with Oscar coming third in the Drivers' Championship. The season's success was a testament to the hard work of the entire team, supported by our valued partners and passionate fans.

The Company has seen revenue increase from £488,393k to £588,483k primarily as a result of increased commercial activity after a strong season of on track performance.

The revenue growth has been offset by increased cost of sales and administrative costs largely due to payments of share based payments, resulting in an operating loss of £42,758k (2024: profit of £48,462k), whilst net interest expense of £1,091k (2024: £14,256k) has reduced year on year due to the repayment of the loan notes in the year resulting in less interest payable. Overall, this has resulted in a loss before tax of £43,849k (2024: profit before tax of £34,206k).

The net loss figure translated to a reduction in cash and cash equivalents balance at year end of £139,702k (2024: £214,014k) additionally influenced from increased financing activities including the repayment of shareholder loan notes in September 2025, and introduction of a new loan.

Net assets fell to £45,948k in 2025 from £60,212k in 2024, predominantly due to the cumulative impact of the repayment of the loan and share based payment liability, offset with the increase in commercial activity and a new loan.

**Other key performance indicators:**

The Directors regularly review multiple KPIs in order to monitor overall business performance. These included the Championship position, as success on track has a direct impact on prize money, sponsorship and stakeholder engagement.

Other areas of focus include monthly review of financial performance against budget and where applicable, prior year, specifically in regards to turnover, operating profit and cash flow which ensure appropriate management focus on the Company's financial health.

There are a number of other non-financial key performance indicators that are important to the Company, the most important that are monitored are around staff performance and reward, core values and partner satisfaction. They are monitored on a routine basis but are reportable to the leadership team who closely review these against previous results and adapt strategies to meet the objectives.

**Future developments & subsequent events:**

2026 will see McLaren look to defend the Constructors' World Championship with the same driver line up of Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris, under a new era of regulations.

From 2027, McLaren Racing will launch its McLaren Racing Endurance Team – McLaren United AS – on the 30th anniversary of its legendary 1995 win on debut at the 24 Hours of Le Mans, with drivers J.J. Lehto, Yannick Dalmas, and Masanori Sekiya. The new Hypercar, designed by McLaren, will be produced by Dallara and run in collaboration with United Autosports, featuring a V6 twin-turbo engine.

Investment in McLaren Racing Limited:
McLaren Racing Limited had a long-term investment since December 2020, led by MSP Sports Capital ("MSP"),
who co-invested with its strategic partners UBS O’Connor, LLC. and The Najafi Companies, whilst an additional
tranche of funding was provided by UBS O'Connor LLC, the investment group Caspian and Ares Capital (ASME
Holdings |, LP). The investment amount was in the form of convertible loan notes and warrants, valued at £179m
at December 2024.
In September 2025 the Company repaid the loan notes and the shares held by the investors were acquired by
McLaren Group Limited (the "Group") granting them full ownership of the Company. The transaction simplified
ownership structure and enabled the ultimate owners to increase their share and assume full ownership, with
Bahrain Murmtalakat Holding Company as majority shareholder and CYVN Holdings-LLC owning a non-
controlling stake in the Group.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Competitive & operational risks:
As ever the key direct. risks and uncertainties faced by the business are continuing to be competitive in all
operations, ability to gain and retain sponsorship, control of the cost base of developing the competitive cars and
staff recruitment and retention.
The Company depends on a large number of suppliers for materials, parts, and services, and many of these are
single source suppliers. Disruptions to supply chains, unavailability of components or a decline in the quality of
goods and services purchased may disrupt or adversely affect operations. The Company has developed close
relationships with suppliers and dealerships to ensure proactive engagement on potential risks and disruptions
and provide regular reporting to senior management and the Board on these
A Company risk register is maintained with direct input from across the business. This aims to highlight relevant
risks and provide focus for mitigations and contingency planning, with emerging issues being escalated to the
Board for discussion.
Financial risk management:
The Company is primarily funded through prize money and its sponsorship agreements, and the risks around
these are determined largely by on track performance. The risks. are managed by optimising results through
investing in both research and development of the car, the team that design, build & race the car and through.
signing medium and long-term agreements with key partners and sponsors.
Cash flows are carefully budgeted and controlled by management to ensure there is sufficient liquidity within the
business to operate through both the short and long-term. The Company has foreign currency dealings in USD,
EUR, SAR and multiple other currencies, Key transactions are reviewed by management who agree policies and
approaches to manage associated risks. around fluctuating FX rates for our prize fund and foreign denominated
sponsorship income. The treasury policy agreed by the Board is to give a level of certainty over the foreign
exchange fluctuations. This is assessed regularly by the Board, with hedging arrangements in place to mitigate
against the largest risks whilst balancing volatility of GBP against both EUR and USD in 2025. Naturally, residual
USD exposures above the hedge designation levels have produced non-favourable results for cash inflows.
