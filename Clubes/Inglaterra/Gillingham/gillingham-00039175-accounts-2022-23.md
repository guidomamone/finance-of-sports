| For The Year Ended 31 May 2023 |
| --- |
| The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 May 2023. |
| --- |
| The principal activity of Gillingham Football Club Limited continues to be the operation of a professional football club together with related commercial activities. |
| --- |
| The club also operates a conferencing and events business. |
| --- |
| The primary aim of the football club and associated businesses is to provide adequate funding to enable success on the field of play. |
| --- |
| REVIEW OF BUSINESS |
| --- |
| Football Review |
| --- |
| The 2022/2023 Season saw the football club return to Sky Bet League Two for the first time since its 2012/2013 Season. The football club was sitting in a relegation position when the majority acquisition of the Company occurred in December 2022. The football club avoided relegation and stayed in the EFL by the end of the 2022/2023 Season. |
| --- |
| Youth Academy |
| --- |
| The Youth Academy is an important part of the football business, with a focus on developing talent coming through different age groups. The football team offered two professional first team contracts to Youth Academy players this season. |
| --- |
| Results and performance |
| --- |
| The results of the company for the year, as set out on pages 5 to 23, show a loss on ordinary activities before tax of £2,148,360 (2022: £904,462). The shareholders' funds of the Company total £623,983 (2022: £11,236.977). Turnover showed an increase to £6.32m (2022: £5.55m) with a slow increase to commercial and catering sales in the period. |
| --- |
| PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES |
| --- |
| There are a number of potential risks and uncertainties which could have a material impact on the Clubs long term performance. These risks and uncertainties are monitored by the Board on a regular basis. |
| --- |
| Compliance with regulation, legal and ethical standards is a priority for the Football Club. |
| --- |
| FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS |
| --- |
| The strategic plan is to stabilise the Company, make the football club financially sustainable, and eventually reach higher levels of the English Football League pyramid. |
