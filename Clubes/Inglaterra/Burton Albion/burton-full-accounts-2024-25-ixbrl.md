Strategic Report

For the Year Ended 30 June 2025
| Introduction |
| --- |

The principal activity of the Company is that of a Professional Football League Club.
| Business review |
| --- |

The 2024/25 season saw Burton Albion Football Club compete in the English Football League (EFL) League One for the seventh consecutive season.
This was the first season the Club competed under the Nordic Football Group ownership. The Club endured a difficult start to the 2024/25 season, spending the entire first half of the campaign in the relegation zone. However, performances began to improve in early 2025, with the team showing greater consistency and resilience. This upturn in form ultimately allowed the club to preserve its League One status for the men’s first team.
Over the course of this period, the Club's introductory board, the ownership group and its advisors have been reviewing all aspects of the organisation to develop a structure on both the sporting and business side that can lay the foundations for the future growth, success and sustainability of Burton Albion.
After the review, a new Board of Directors was formed in May 2025 with the appointment of Wouter Gudde taking up the role of Interim Chairman and CEO. Working alongside Wouter as the ownership’s representatives on the club’s board also includes Ólafur Páll Snorrason and Robert Ulfarsson. Ólafur has led NFG's Sporting Committee and acted as NFG’s interim Sporting Director since the departure of Bendik Hareide. Robert is a member of NFG’s Investor Advisory Board since before NFG came in as custodians of the club.
As a result, Chairman Ole Jakob Strandhagen, Deputy Chairman Tom Davidson, and Commercial Director Kevin C. Skabo will step down from their positions on the board, following on from the previous announcement around the departure of fellow board member and Sporting Director Bendik Hareide in 2024.
Since May 2025, under the guidance of a newly formed board and the appointment of Tom Mahon and Richard Dorman as directors, Burton Albion FC has taken decisive steps to restructure its operations and implement a more sustainable financial model.
The financial results for the year ended 30 June 2025 of the Company are set out on pages 10 to 29. The Company has achieved a modest revenue growth to £6.4m compared to the year ending June 2024.
The Company made a loss of £8.3m for the year (2024: £1.3m loss), reflecting investment in the playing squad and associated operating costs following the change in ownership. This investment was funded through additional financial support from the ownership group, with funding advanced during the year to support the Club’s operations and strategic objectives. Subsequent to the year end, this funding was formally waived and treated as a capital contribution by the ownership group.
The board will continue to seek the right balance between financial investment and on-pitch success, while providing the best possible community facilities—not only for the men’s senior team but for all teams at Burton Albion Football Club, as well as for the local community to enjoy.
The Club has always been, and continues to be, dedicated to its staff, its fan base, and the local community and businesses in the surrounding areas. The Club is appreciative of the continual support from its supporters, season ticket holders, Vice Presidents and all of its sponsors.
