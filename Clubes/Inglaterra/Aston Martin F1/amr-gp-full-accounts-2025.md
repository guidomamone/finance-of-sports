Principal activities and business review
The principal activity of the Company is to operate a Formula One racing team.
The Company carries out that activity through the design, development, manufacture, testing and racing of
cars for participation in the FIA Formula One World Championship.
The Company's success is measured by its performance in the Constructors’ and Drivers’ World
Championships and by reference to the financial parameters defined by its shareholders. The Company's
strategic objectives are to optimise its performance in the Constructors’ and Drivers' World Championships,
and to build a team capable of winning both Championships.
Formula One
2025 marked the fourth year of the current technical regulations, with 24 feature races and 6 sprint races over
the season. The result for 2025 was a total of 89 points and a 7th place finish in the Constructors’
Championship.
For 2026, the team are retaining Fernando Alonso and Lance Stroll as our race drivers. 2026 represents the
first season of a new set of technical regulations with significant changes to both the engine and aerodynamic
regulations.
The team continued its participation in the all-female F1 Academy series in 2025, with Swiss driver Tina
Hausmann’s second and final season with the team, finishing 8th overall in the championship. For 2026,
Mathilda Paatz will join the team, after her previous success in F4 Championship.
Commercial
Away from the track the team continued to progress, with new partnerships contributing to a 23% increase in
revenue. Most of these partnerships are multi-year agreements, highlighting the strength of the Aston Martin
brand and the commitment of our partners in driving performance both on and off the track.
Revenue is expected to continue to grow in future years; for 2026 the team has signed multi-year deals with
Cognition, Cohere, Breitling, CoinPayments and Eight Sleep.

Infrastructure and investment
Following the completion of Phase Two and Three of the new AMR Technology Campus in 2024, the Wind
Powered Tunnel, which formed part of Phase Three, was fully commissioned in 2025.
As the team's FIA nominated wind tunnel, it has been used to support the development of the AMR25, but with
its primary focus on AMR26. The use of our own wind tunnel marks a significant step in the Team's journey
towards challenging for championships in the future.
In addition to the above, the Company secured an additional borrowing of $40m to allow further growth and
expansion as the team and the sport continues to grow in both size and complexity.
Turnover for the year was £344 million (2024 - £281 million) and the Company made a loss after taxation of
£28.5 million (2024 - £46 million).
In 2026 and beyond the Company will continue with its principal activities as described above.
