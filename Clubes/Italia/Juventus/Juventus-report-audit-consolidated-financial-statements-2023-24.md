REPORT ON THE AUDIT OF THE CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Qualified opinion

We have audited the consolidated financial statements of Juventus Football Club S.p.A. and its
subsidiaries (the “Juventus Group” or the “Group”), which comprise the statement of financial position
as of June 30, 2024, the income statement, the statement of comprehensive income, the statement of
changes in shareholders’ equity, the statement of cash flows for the year then ended and notes to the
financial statements, including a summary of significant accounting policies.

In our opinion, except for the effects on the prior year information, presented for comparative purposes,
of the matters described in the “Effects on prior year information presented for comparative purposes of
measure regarding the remuneration of registered personnel for the 2020/21 season” and “Effects on
prior year information presented for comparative purposes of transactions with other football clubs
relating to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020” paragraphs of the “Basis for qualified opinion” section of our
report and the possible effects on the prior year information presented for comparative purposes of the
matter described in the “Possible effects on prior year information presented for comparative purposes of
further transactions with other football clubs relating to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020” paragraph of
the same section, the consolidated financial statements give a true and fair view of the financial position
of the Group as of June 30, 2024, and of its result and cash flows for the year then ended in accordance
with International Financial Reporting Standards as adopted by the European Union and with the
requirements of national regulations issued pursuant to art. 9 of Italian Legislative Decree no. 38/05.

Basis for qualified opinion

Effects on prior year information presented for comparative purposes of measure regarding the
remuneration of registered personnel for the 2020/21 season

Our audit report on the consolidated financial statements of Juventus Football Club S.p.A. (hereinafter,
also the “Issuer” or the “Company”) as of June 30, 2022 and as of June 30, 2023 included a qualification
with regard to the failure to recognize in the related financial years the accounting effects of the
measures regarding the remuneration of registered personnel for the 2019/20 and 2020/21 seasons (this
last one hereinafter “2020/21 salary manoeuvre”).

In relation to this issue, as a consequence of the criteria adopted for the recognition of the 2020/21
salary manoeuvre in past years, we believe that the loss for the prior year ended June 30, 2023,
information on which is presented for comparative purposes, is overstated by Euro 5 million.

The Report on Operations and Notes 55 and 57 of the notes to the financial statements contain
disclosures on the proceedings involving the Company with regard to the measures regarding the
remuneration of registered personnel for the 2019/20 and 2020/21 seasons.

The audit procedures on the aforementioned salary manoeuvres were carried out by us in the context of
the audit of the Issuer’s statutory and consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2022 and 2023.
As part of the audit of the consolidated financial statements as of June 30, 2024, we verified the effects
of the qualification expressed in our previous audit reports on the data for the year ended June 30, 2023
presented for comparative purposes.

Effects on prior year information presented for comparative purposes of transactions with other football
clubs relating to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020

Our audit report on the consolidated financial statements of the Issuer as of June 30, 2023 reported the
existence of certain memoranda, notes and agreements (hereinafter referred to as "side letters") related
to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020, that had not been provided to us during our audit of the Issuer's
statutory and consolidated financial statements for the year ended June 30, 2022, mainly regarding
transactions with other football teams carried out by the Company in previous financial years, and
included a qualification with regard to the erroneous recognition in the financial statements of the
Company for the year ended June 30, 2019 of a capital gain on one of the aforesaid transactions with
other football teams (hereinafter referred to as "transactions with other football clubs 2018, 2019 and
2020").

If the Company had not recognized the aforesaid capital gain, also as a result of subsequent events
regarding the repurchase of the player and, ultimately, his sale to a third team, the loss for the prior year
ended June 30, 2023, information on which is presented for comparative purposes, would have been
lower by Euro 7 million.

The audit work performed by us in relation to this matter and the references to the disclosures reported
in the Report on Operations and in the notes to the financial statements are described in the following
paragraph.

Possible effects on prior year information presented for comparative purposes of further transactions
with other football clubs relating to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020

With reference to the other side letters mentioned in the previous paragraph “Effects on prior year
information presented for comparative purposes of transactions with other football clubs relating to the
years 2018, 2019 and 2020”, our aforementioned audit report include a scope limitation to our audit
procedures as the information obtained from them and from the additional evidence acquired by us was
incomplete in that, among other things, it did not provide evidence regarding the consent of the
registered players, a circumstance necessary, in our opinion, for us to be able to conclude on the
existence of enforceable rights and obligations within the meaning of IFRS 15 (c.d. “enforceability”) of
said agreements relating to the years 2018, 2019 and 2020.

Responsibility of the Directors and the Board of Statutory Auditors for the Consolidated Financial
Statements

The Directors are responsible for the preparation of consolidated financial statements that give a true
and fair view in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards as adopted by the European
Union and the requirements of national regulations issued pursuant to art. 9 of Italian Legislative Decree
no. 38/05 and, within the terms established by law, for such internal control as the Directors determine
is necessary to enable the preparation of financial statements that are free from material misstatement,
whether due to fraud or error.

In preparing the consolidated financial statements, the Directors are responsible for assessing the
Group’s ability to continue as a going concern, disclosing, as applicable, matters related to going concern
and using the going concern basis of accounting unless they have identified the existence of the
conditions for the liquidation of the of the parent company Juventus Football Club S.p.A. or for the
termination of the operations or have no realistic alternative to such choices.

The Board of Statutory Auditors is responsible for overseeing, within the terms established by law, the
Group’s financial reporting process.

Auditor’s Responsibility for the Audit of the Consolidated Financial Statements

Our objectives are to obtain reasonable assurance about whether the consolidated financial statements
as a whole are free from material misstatement, whether due to fraud or error, and to issue an auditor’s
report that includes our opinion. Reasonable assurance is a high level of assurance but is not a guarantee
that an audit conducted in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA Italia) will always
detect a material misstatement when it exists. Misstatements can arise from fraud or error and are
considered material if, individually or in the aggregate, they could reasonably be expected to influence
the economic decisions of users taken on the basis of these consolidated financial statements.

As part of an audit in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (ISA Italia), we exercise
professional judgment and maintain professional skepticism throughout the audit. We also:

   identify and assess the risks of material misstatement of the consolidated financial statements,
    whether due to fraud or error, design and perform audit procedures responsive to those risks, and
    obtain audit evidence that is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for our opinion. The risk of
    not detecting a material misstatement resulting from fraud is higher than for one resulting from
    error, as fraud may involve collusion, forgery, intentional omissions, misrepresentations, or the
    override of internal control.
